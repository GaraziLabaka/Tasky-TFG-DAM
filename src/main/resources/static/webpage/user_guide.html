User Guide

Welcome to the Tasky User Guide! This guide will help you get started with using Tasky, a task management application designed to help you organize your tasks efficiently.

Getting Started

To get started with Tasky, follow these steps:

  1. Create an Account: Sign up for a new account using your email address and a secure password.
  2. Log In: Log in to your account to access your dashboard.
  3. Create Tasks: Start writing in the fields and click the "Add Task" button. The task will appear on a list.
  4. Delete Tasks: Select a task and click the "Delete Task" button to remove it from your list.
  5. Edit Tasks: Click on a task and then click the "Edit Task" button to edit its details, such as the title, description, category or date. Click the "Save Task" button to save your changes.
  6. Track Progress: Mark tasks as complete when finished and track your progress over time.

Features

  • Task Management: Create, edit, and delete tasks with ease.
  • Remember your tasks: Never forget an important task with our reminder feature. Click the "Notify by mail" button to receive notifications or let the app notify you whenever you want.
  • Organize with Categories: Categorize your tasks to keep them organized and easily accessible.
  • Search and Filter: Easily find tasks using the search and filter options. Write a title or date and press Enter. Choose a category and click on it to filter your data.
